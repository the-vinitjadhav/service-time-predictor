How It Works

This machine learning model predicts service turnaround times based on:

  • Car Condition: Better condition typically means faster service
  • Service Type: Complexity of the required service
  • Staff Experience: More experienced staff work more efficiently
  • Spare Parts: Availability of required parts
  • Workload: Current shop workload affects scheduling

The model was trained on thousands of simulated service scenarios using Random Forest regression.
